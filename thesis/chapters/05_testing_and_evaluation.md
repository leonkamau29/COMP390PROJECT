# Chapter 5: Testing and Evaluation

**Target length:** 2,500–4,000 words (Phases 4 + toolkit pilot)
**Guide section:** "Testing & Evaluation" — evaluating whether the project outputs meet the original aims
**Content source:** Phase 4 outputs (expert validation) + Phase 5 toolkit pilot-test results

---

> **Note on structure:** In this research project, "testing and evaluation" takes two forms: (1) expert validation of the framework and findings through a structured survey (Phase 4), which evaluates whether the taxonomy, coverage analysis, and gap rankings are judged sound by knowledgeable practitioners; and (2) pilot testing of the Assessment Toolkit (Phase 5) with non-expert colleagues, which evaluates usability. Both are distinct from the researcher's self-reflection, which appears in Chapter 7.
