## 9.1 BCS Criteria

The following table maps this project against the six outcomes required by the Chartered Institute for IT (BCS).

| # | BCS Outcome | Where addressed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ability to apply practical and analytical skills gained during the degree programme | Chapters 3 and 4 (thematic analysis, statistical testing, Python scripting) |
| 2 | Innovation and/or creativity | Chapter 6 (five original benchmark designs; practitioner toolkit) |
| 3 | Synthesis of information, ideas and practices to provide a quality solution, with evaluation | Chapters 4–5 (coverage analysis synthesising 131 tasks, 28 benchmarks, 3 statistical tests; expert validation) |
| 4 | That the project meets a real need in a wider context | Chapter 1 (motivation from Handa et al. 2025; industry deployment decisions) |
| 5 | Ability to self-manage a significant piece of work | See Section 9.2 below |
| 6 | Critical self-evaluation of the process | See Section 9.3 below |
